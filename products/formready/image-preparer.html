What the checker can verify

FormReady measures the exported format, pixel dimensions and file size against the values shown. It cannot tell whether the person in the photo is the applicant, whether a photo is recent, whether a signature is genuine or legible enough, or whether a portal’s rules have changed.

Presets show their source and cycleSome published exam cycles are over. A preset is clearly labeled when it is historical; always follow the newest notice and application portal. File checks do not guarantee application acceptance.

FormReady is an independent utility and is not affiliated with any government agency or examination board. Image processing stays in your browser.
