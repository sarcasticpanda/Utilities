About FormReady

FormReady is an independent image-preparation utility for applicants who need to crop, resize and check a photo or signature against requirements they already have.

Enter the required dimensions, output format and maximum file size from the current official application instructions. FormReady measures the generated file and reports what matches.

It is not affiliated with a government agency or examination board, does not guarantee acceptance, and does not invent application presets.
