Privacy

The selected image is decoded, cropped and exported in your browser; the tool code does not upload it or save it in local storage. Temporary previews and generated results remain in the page until you use Clear image and result or close the page. The hosting provider receives ordinary HTTP request data needed to serve the site. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives the request needed to deliver that font. FormReady has no account system, analytics, or advertising enabled.
