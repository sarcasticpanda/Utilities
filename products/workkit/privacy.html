Privacy

Shift times, the hourly rate and the overtime threshold are processed in your browser. The timesheet draft is stored in this browser local storage on this device so it can be restored. Use Clear if you want to remove the current draft; browser storage may remain until cleared in your browser settings. For overtime currency conversion, the browser requests a daily exchange rate from Frankfurter using only the selected currency codes; hours, hourly rates and pay amounts remain in your browser. WorkKit does not have an account system, analytics, or advertising enabled. The hosting provider receives ordinary HTTP request data needed to serve the site. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives the request needed to deliver that font. Avoid shared devices if you do not want a local draft to remain.
