Privacy

Shift times, the hourly rate and the overtime threshold are processed in your browser. The timesheet draft is stored in this browser local storage on this device so it can be restored. Use Clear if you want to remove the current draft; browser storage may remain until cleared in your browser settings. For overtime currency conversion, the browser requests a daily exchange rate from Frankfurter using only the selected currency codes; hours, hourly rates and pay amounts remain in your browser. WorkKit does not have an account system, analytics, or advertising enabled. The hosting provider receives ordinary HTTP request data needed to serve the site. The Outfit typeface is served from this site. Avoid shared devices if you do not want a local draft to remain.

Free work calculators for hours, pay and scheduling.

Advertising and cookies: Advertising is not currently enabled on this site. If ads are enabled later, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar storage to serve and measure ads, including ads based on visits to this site and other sites. You can manage Google ad personalization in Google Ads Settings and review opt-out choices for participating vendors at AboutAds. Before enabling ads, this policy will be updated with the applicable vendors and consent information; required consent controls will be in place for the regions served.
