How it works

Each shift is calculated in minutes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, WorkKit treats it as an overnight shift. Break minutes are subtracted before total and overtime hours are calculated. Gross pay uses the rate entered above and is an estimate before taxes or deductions. Choose a currency matching your entered rate; WorkKit does not exchange currencies.

Does WorkKit upload my timesheet?

No. Shift details are saved in local browser storage on this device and are not sent to a WorkKit server.

How is overtime estimated?

This calculator uses your entered weekly threshold and a 1.5× rate for overtime. Workplace rules can differ, so confirm the applicable policy.
