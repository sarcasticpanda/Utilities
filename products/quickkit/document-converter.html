Home/Document converter

Convert Word and PDF files

Prepare a DOCX for PDF, or turn selectable PDF text into an editable Word file. Files stay in this browser.

DOCX → PDF

Word to PDF

Preview your Word content, then use your browser’s print dialog to save it as a PDF.

The browser rebuilds the document from its text and common formatting. Review the preview before saving.

Complex columns, floating text, tracked changes and exact page layout may differ. In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF.”

PDF → DOCX

PDF to Word

Extract selectable PDF text into a new, editable Word document.

Scanned pages need OCR first in the PDF editor.

Text and page order are kept when available. PDF columns, images, tables and exact formatting may need cleanup in Word.

Your documents stay on this device

Both tools process the selected file in your browser. Word-to-PDF uses the browser’s print dialog; PDF-to-Word reads the PDF text layer. The converter does not upload or store your files.
