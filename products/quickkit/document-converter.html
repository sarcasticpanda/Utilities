Home/Document converter

Convert Word and PDF files

Prepare a DOCX for PDF, or turn selectable and OCR-recognized PDF text into an editable Word file. Files stay in this browser.

DOCX → PDF

Word to PDF

Preview your Word content, then use your browser’s print dialog to save it as a PDF.

The browser rebuilds the document from its text and common formatting. Review the preview before saving.

Complex columns, floating text, tracked changes and exact page layout may differ. In the print dialog, choose “Save as PDF.”

PDF → DOCX

PDF to Word

Extract selectable text and recognize scanned pages, then review the result before creating an editable Word file.

Selectable text is extracted automatically. Image-only pages are recognized in this browser; check OCR text before export.

Review recognized pages

OCR can misread names, numbers and punctuation. Correct the text below before creating the Word file.

English OCR runs on up to 30 scanned pages per file. Text order is preserved where possible; columns, handwriting, images, tables and exact formatting may need manual cleanup in Word.

Your documents stay on this device

Both tools process the selected file in your browser. Word-to-PDF uses the browser’s print dialog; PDF text extraction and OCR run on this device. The OCR engine downloads English recognition data from a public CDN, but your document is not uploaded.
