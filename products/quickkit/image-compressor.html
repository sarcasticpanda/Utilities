How target-size compression works

QuickKit reduces image quality and, when needed, dimensions until the exported file is at or below your selected limit. The final byte size is measured after processing. Some images cannot meet a very small target while preserving useful detail; in that case, the result will say so.

Private by design

The selected image is decoded and exported locally by your browser. QuickKit does not need to upload it for compression.
