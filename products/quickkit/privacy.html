Privacy

Image, PDF and video files, photos, GPS coordinates, pasted text, Wi-Fi details and generated passwords are handled in your browser and are not uploaded to QuickKit or saved in local storage. The geo-tag photo tool asks before using the camera or current location; GPS coordinates are embedded only when you create a JPEG, and anyone who receives it may inspect that metadata. Opening the optional map sends tile requests and the visible map area to OpenStreetMap. If you print a map card, creating the photo requests the nearby map tiles for the selected location, which reveals that approximate area to OpenStreetMap. A place search sends your typed query to OpenStreetMap only when you press Search; Use current location and Find place name send exact coordinates to OpenStreetMap for a nearby name when pressed. OpenStreetMap may process these requests under its privacy policy. Opening a point in Google Maps sends its coordinates to Google in a new tab. Photo files remain in your browser. Temporary previews and generated outputs are cleared when you use the tool reset controls or close the page. The hosting provider receives ordinary HTTP request data needed to serve the site. The Outfit typeface is served from this site. If you choose OCR, the browser downloads the Tesseract worker and selected language model from jsDelivr; if you choose PDF source-text editing, it downloads a qpdf WebAssembly engine from QuickKit. Your PDF stays local in both cases. QuickKit has no account system, analytics, or advertising enabled. Avoid entering sensitive information on a shared device.
