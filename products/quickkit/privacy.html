Privacy

Image and PDF files, pasted text, Wi-Fi details and generated passwords are handled in your browser by the tool code; these tools do not upload them to QuickKit or save them in local storage. Temporary previews and generated outputs are cleared when you use the tool reset controls or close the page. The hosting provider receives ordinary HTTP request data needed to serve the site. The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which receives the request needed to deliver that font. QuickKit has no account system, analytics, or advertising enabled. Avoid entering sensitive information on a shared device.
