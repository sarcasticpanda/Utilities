        What to expect

        QuickKit uses codecs available in your browser. A trim, crop, join or compression re-encodes the video and may reduce quality. Supported input formats depend on the codec inside the file, not just its filename. The tool will show an error if this browser cannot process it.

        Use Trim to keep a time range or remove one; use Crop to frame the video as widescreen, classic, square or portrait. In Edit and join, reorder clips, add sections from the same source, and place soundtrack files along the joined timeline.
