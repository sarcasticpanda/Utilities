# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: This workspace had no application scaffold. A multi-page static HTML/CSS/JavaScript build with Vite was selected to keep the three properties separately deployable, fast to launch, and usable without application servers. Browser libraries are bundled locally.

## Users

- **WorkKit:** hourly workers, freelancers, managers and small businesses calculating hours, time cards, gross pay and overtime.
- **QuickKit:** people who need a small image, PDF, QR or text task completed quickly on a phone or computer.
- **FormReady:** India-first applicants, students, families and cyber-cafe operators preparing a photo or signature for an online application.

These audiences and jobs come from `UTILITY_SITES_COMPLETE_MASTER.md`.

## Product Purpose

Build three separate, free utility websites for common work-time, file-processing, and online-application image tasks. Each should make its core task easy to complete without an account and be useful on desktop and mobile. The user's goal is to publish them and pursue future side income; traffic and revenue are not guaranteed by publishing.

## Positioning

- **WorkKit:** connected hours, time-card and gross-pay calculations, so workers can enter shifts once and carry the result into overtime or pay tools.
- **QuickKit:** focused, no-account browser utilities for small tasks, with exact-size image compression as a distinctive flow.
- **FormReady:** prepare an image against requirements the user enters from current official instructions, with a clear validation result and local processing.

## Operating Context

Visitors often arrive from a specific search for a small task, want the control immediately, and may be on a phone or under an application deadline. Tools should give clear results, meaningful error recovery, and a direct download or export when appropriate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Browser-side processing is preferred for calculations and files; no user account or persistent file uploads are required.
- WorkKit pay values are gross estimates, not payroll, tax, legal or employment advice.
- QuickKit should launch a focused set of working tools rather than a large catalog of thin pages.
- FormReady requirements must be user-entered or stored as data with official sources. Do not guess or claim exam affiliation. An empty preset list is preferable to unverified requirements.
- Reused upstream source requires an inspected current license and retained attribution. Current code is an independent implementation using packaged libraries; do not copy upstream branding or marketing.
- Search metadata, sitemap and canonical URLs must be set to real production origins before publication. Do not leave placeholder domains.
- Public contact details, production domains and hosting accounts are not present in the workspace yet.
- Current names are working product names. The user has not approved permanent brand names or supplied custom domains.

## Brand Commitments

The product pack uses the working names WorkKit, QuickKit and FormReady. FormReady must never imply official government or examination-board status. Tone should be practical, clear and trustworthy.

- No domain ownership, public contact address, official application preset sources, testimonials, traffic results, ad-network approval or customer research was provided. Do not invent these.

## Product Principles

1. Complete the visitor's stated task with little friction.
2. Be accurate about outputs, limits and privacy.
3. Keep the core tools free and account-free.
4. Make mobile use a first-class path.
5. Publish only pages with useful content and working tools.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic labels, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, clear text status (not color alone), reduced-motion support, responsive layouts and touch-friendly controls.
