# WorkKit and FormReady competitor review

Research checked 30 October 2026. Traffic figures below are third-party estimates, not competitor analytics, and can vary by provider and month.

## WorkKit: time cards and work-hour estimates

### Reference sites

- [Calculator.net](https://www.calculator.net/) is a very large calculator publisher. Semrush estimated 67.51 million total site visits in September 2026. The figure is for the whole domain, not for its time-card page. Its audience is useful evidence for calculator-style search demand, not a promise that a new time-card tool will receive comparable traffic. [Semrush estimate](https://www.semrush.com/website/calculator.net/overview/)
- [QuickBooks Time Card Calculator](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/time-tracking/resources/time-card-calculator/) provides weekly/biweekly periods, five/seven-day weeks, daily/weekly overtime options, an overtime multiplier, base pay, breaks, and CSV/print export.
- [TimeCardCentral](https://timecardcentral.com/en/time-card-calculator/) demonstrates a deeper work-hour workflow: multiple breaks, per-day rates, employee tabs, overnight shifts, CSV/PDF/print and shareable sheets. These are product reference points; WorkKit should keep the simpler solo-worker entry path.
- [TimecardCalculator.net](https://timecardcalculator.net/) is a focused domain, but available third-party estimates were around 250,000 visits/month in August 2026, not millions. [HypeStat estimate](https://hypestat.com/info/timecardcalculator.net)

### Recommended WorkKit improvements

1. Add a compact, optional settings area for week length (5/7 days), weekly/biweekly view and time display.
2. Add a one-tap “copy previous shift/day” action and clear rows/period labels.
3. Add a print-friendly timesheet so people can print or save to PDF without creating an account.
4. Consider optional rounding and overtime settings only when the UI clearly explains that rules vary by employer and location. Do not present a generic result as payroll/legal advice.
5. Keep the existing CSV export and local-only draft saving; make estimates and storage behavior visible next to the tool.

## FormReady: exact photo/signature preparation

### Reference sites

- [ResizePixel](https://www.resizepixel.com/en) lists crop, resize, rotate, mirror, compress-to-size, convert, pixelate and black-and-white tools. Semrush estimated 4.95 million visits in September 2026 for the whole domain. [Semrush estimate](https://www.semrush.com/website/resizepixel.com/overview/)
- [ImResizer](https://imresizer.com/image-tools) groups resize/compress by pixel and physical units, custom KB/MB targets, photo templates, country-specific templates, and shareable prefilled settings links. Semrush estimated 2.29 million visits in September 2026. [Semrush estimate](https://www.semrush.com/website/imresizer.com/overview/)

### Recommended FormReady improvements

1. Convert millimeters/inches plus DPI to pixels, while keeping pixel inputs available.
2. Add rotate-left, rotate-right and reset-crop controls beside the crop preview.
3. Support a small set of user-saved local presets for frequently reused dimensions and size limits; mark them user-defined, not officially approved.
4. Explain when requested dimensions and file-size limits cannot both be met. Never label a photo as government-, exam- or biometric-compliant unless a current official source supports that exact preset.
5. Keep crop, compression and export on-device, and state accepted formats and input limits before upload.

## Implementation boundary

“Visits” means sessions, not ad clicks, page clicks or unique people. The estimates are month-specific and may differ from first-party analytics. The live public pages were reviewed for user flows, controls and publicly stated features. We will reproduce useful behavior with WorkKit/FormReady's own layout, copy and implementation. Do not reuse competitor code, images, branding, or proprietary text. Traffic estimates do not guarantee our traffic or advertising income.
